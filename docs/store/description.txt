Illini Dash puts every UIUC deadline in one place.

Canvas, Gradescope, PrairieLearn, PrairieTest and smartPhysics each hold part of your
semester, and none of them shows you the others. Checking five sites to find out what is
due this week is how a deadline gets missed — not because it was hidden, but because it
was somewhere you didn't look that day.

Illini Dash reads all of them and puts what it finds on one calendar, in your browser,
using the logins you already have. Click the icon and you can see the week.

WHAT IT DOES

• Day, week and month views, plus a list of just the things that need attention. Your
  courses are colour-coded, and any course can be switched off with one click.

• One entry per assignment. Work posted in Gradescope and mirrored into Canvas shows up
  once, linked to the place you actually submit it — not twice, from two sites that
  disagree about the title.

• Exams in their own place, with the room and how long each one runs, including the ones
  further out than the calendar reaches.

• CBTF exams you have reserved, and a daily reminder about exams that are open for
  booking and not yet reserved — the deadline that has no deadline on it.

• Reminders 24 hours and 2 hours before something is due, with quiet hours so nothing
  wakes you at 3am.

• Reduced-credit deadlines. PrairieLearn's "80% until Tuesday" is a real deadline, and it
  is shown as one.

• Your own course websites. Plenty of courses keep the real schedule on their own page.
  Paste the address, see exactly what was found, and add it once it looks right.

• Calendar export. Save everything as an .ics file from the toolbar, or send a single
  deadline to Google Calendar.

NO ACCOUNT, NO PASSWORD, NO SERVER

Illini Dash reads the pages you are already signed in to, the same way your browser would
if you opened them yourself, and keeps everything on your own device. It never sees a
password. Nothing is uploaded anywhere, because there is nowhere to upload it to — there
is no server, no account and no analytics. Uninstalling it deletes everything it stored.
