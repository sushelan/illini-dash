Colour, rebuilt on one system

Same September, same rows. Three schemes that are variations of one palette rather than three separate moods.

What was wrong before. The course colours came from a generic UI library, so they had no relationship to each other or to the brand — and picked in hex, they were not even equally bright, so one course shouted and another whispered at random. Worst of all they were as saturated as the warnings, so which course a thing belongs to competed with whether it is late. A course is a label. Being late is a meaning. Below, labels sit at one fixed lightness and low chroma, meanings get more than double the chroma, and that gap is the entire hierarchy.
