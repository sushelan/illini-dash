## Brand & Style

This design system draws inspiration from 19th-century European scholars' compendiums, French Seyès ruled notebooks, and bespoke leather-bound journals. It combines the tactile romance of physical stationery—parchment tooth, iron gall ink bleeding slightly into fibrous cream pages, brass clips, and sealing wax—with the high-density utility required of a modern academic and personal planner.

### Design Movement: Tactile Literary Skeuomorphism & Scholarly Editorial
The interface avoids cold digital flats in favor of subtle, organic physical cues:
- **Parchment Surfaces:** Tinted layered grounds evocative of vellum, laid paper, and aged linen stock.
- **Ink & Pigment Semantics:** Text and interactive indicators mimic archival fountain pen inks (ferro-gallic blue-black, raw sepia, madder terracotta, and dried sage botanicals).
- **Physical Accents:** Structural accents emulate brass binder clips, cloth ribbon page markers, debossed ruling, and wax cartouches without descending into kitsch.

### Target Audience & Emotional Tone
Designed for academics, writers, classicists, and thoughtful journal keepers. The emotional response is centered, contemplative, disciplined, and warm—turning digital organization into an archival ritual.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in historic artist and scribe materials, balanced to meet WCAG AA legibility across parchment grounds.

### Primary: Iron Gall Ink (`#1a2744`)
The primary voice of authority, structure, and text. A deep blue-black reminiscent of oxidized iron-gall ink on cotton paper. Used for primary typography, dominant buttons, major rules, and high-emphasis states.

### Secondary: Bistre / Sepia (`#4a3b32`)
A warm, smoky walnut sepia. Serves as the structural foil to the deep blue: secondary headings, metadata, tabular borders, and subdued icons.

### Accent: Madder Red / Sealing Wax (`#9e3d24`)
A rich, desaturated cinnabar/terracotta stamp tone. Reserved for destructive actions, pinned alerts, active date indicators, wax-seal badges, and focal callouts.

### Botanical Auxiliary: Dried Sage (`#3f5945`)
A quiet laurel green representing completed tasks, verified citations, botanical margins, and peaceful progress markers.

### Ground & Surfaces: Cream & Vellum
- **Canvas Base (Vellum Virgin):** `#faf6ee`
- **Surface Tier 1 (Aged Folio):** `#f3ede2`
- **Surface Tier 2 (Pressed Card / Margins):** `#ebe2d3`
- **Seyès / Field Grid Rules:** `#d8cfbe` (with primary Seyès guide lines tinted in `#c9bfad`)
- **Ruled Margin Guide:** `#dfa495` (evoking the faint rose vertical margin line of classical French student notebooks)

## Typography

The type system pairs the intellectual stature of **EB Garamond** with **Newsreader** for disciplined metadata and labels.

### Hierarchy & Style Nuance
- **Titling & Journal Folios:** Headlines leverage old-style figures and natural serif contrast. Headlines feel chiseled yet ink-rendered.
- **Editorial Body Baseline:** The line height of `body-md` (26px) is mapped to match notebook ruling grids, allowing reading text to naturally register with ruled background lines when toggled.
- **Annotations & Marginalia:** The `annotation-italic` level captures cursive/handwriting marginal notes. It utilizes the flowing, humanist true-italics of EB Garamond, set slightly lighter in tone (using sepia `#4a3b32` or sage `#3f5945`) as if inscribed with a fine-point dip pen.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Newsreader in small caps or tracking-spaced uppercase conveys the cataloging system of an antique library registry.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around the anatomy of an open codex or bound student ledger.

### Layout Model: The Open Folio Grid
- **Desktop (1024px+):** Emulates a two-page open notebook spread with an authentic center gutter gutter (`2.5rem`). Left page contains indexation, agenda trees, or syllabi; right page houses active entries, ruled compositions, and freeform marginalia. Margins are generous (`2rem` - `3rem`), paying homage to the bookbinder's trim margins.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** Collapses to a single-folio view. Secondary tools tuck into a vintage pull-out ribbon drawer. Outer margin sets to `1.5rem`.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** A single pocket-notebook layout (`margin: 1rem`), honoring the proportions of a French pocket notebook with a faint red margin guide anchored 36px from the left edge.

### Rhythm & Alignment
Vertical rhythm is locked to a 4px base step, scaling through a harmonious 8px/16px/24px/32px vertical cadence that matches Seyès rule increments (8px auxiliary height, 24px major rule height).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects harsh digital drop shadows and neo-brutalist solid blocks, relying instead on physical paper physics, debossing, and warm light casting.

### 1. The Parchment Plate Hierarchy
- **Ground Floor (Base Ledger):** `#faf6ee` base with an ultra-fine repeating organic grain and optional faded ruled lines.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Tipped-in Pages, Leaflets, Cards):** Rendered in `#f3ede2`. Uses an ambient, dual-layer contact shadow tinted with warm sepia: `0 1px 3px rgba(74, 59, 50, 0.08), 0 4px 12px rgba(74, 59, 50, 0.04)`.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Open Modals, Scribe Drawers):** Rendered with a warm, diffuse spread: `0 8px 24px rgba(26, 39, 68, 0.08), 0 2px 6px rgba(74, 59, 50, 0.05)`.

### 2. Debossed Rules & Inset Engravings
Dividers and field borders do not sit flat; they are visually pressed into the paper using a 1px solid stroke in `#d8cfbe` accompanied by a 1px lower highlight of `#ffffff` at 60% opacity.

### 3. Tactile Hardware Accents
Pinned elements and bookmarks introduce tactile, metallic brass highlights (`#c49a45` gradient to `#8f6b28`) or cloth ribbon effects, casting crisp micro-shadows (`0 1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.25)`) directly across the folio page.

## Shapes

The geometry reflects cut stationery, bound book corners, and clipped paper slips. 

### Corner Radius Strategy
- **Base Level (`1` / Soft):** Interactive buttons, input fields, cards, and dialogue containers use subtle 4px (`0.25rem`) corners. This recreates the slight, natural wear and rounded fiber of guillotine-trimmed archival paper rather than synthetic digital rounds.
- **Cards & Folio Plates (`rounded-lg`):** Maximize at 8px (`0.5rem`) to maintain architectural dignity.
- **Specialty Shapes:**
  - **Wax Seal Emblems:** Circular (`50%` roundness) with subtle scalloped, hand-stamped irregular borders.
  - **Brass Bookmark Ribbon:** Straight-edged with an inverted chevron (swallowtail) bottom termination.
  - **Index Tabs:** Top-only rounded corners (`0.25rem 0.25rem 0 0`), emerging naturally from card tops like leatherette dividers.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action (Ink Fill):** Background of Iron Gall Blue (`#1a2744`), text in parchment virgin (`#faf6ee`). Border: 1px solid `#131e34`. Subtle inner top highlight. On hover, the color deepens slightly with a soft blue-black wash (`#121b2f`).
- **Secondary Action (Sepia Quill):** Background transparent or `#f3ede2`, border 1px solid `#4a3b32` with text in `#4a3b32`. Hover introduces a warm aged-wash tint (`#ebe2d3`).
- **Wax Seal Action (Crucial / Submit):** Terracotta crimson (`#9e3d24`), embossed serif lettering, subtle inner shadow mimicking stamped red wax.

### 2. Cards & Folio Panes
- Grounded in `#f3ede2`. 
- Outlined with a fine 1px antique rule `#d8cfbe`.
- Top-right corner may feature an optional brass clip motif or folded parchment dog-ear.
- Headers are separated with a classical double-line rule: a 1px line in sepia, a 2px gap, and an ultra-thin 0.5px subordinate line.

### 3. Notebook Lines & Inputs
- **Text Inputs:** Free of boxed gray rectangles. Instead, inputs rest directly upon an antique ruled line (`border-bottom: 1px solid #d8cfbe`). 
- **Focus State:** The underline transitions to Iron Gall Blue (`#1a2744`), and a faint blue quill caret pulses.
- **Notebook Body Area:** Optional background pattern consisting of horizontal Seyès ruling (spaced at 26px increments in `#e5decfa8`) intersected by the classical single vertical red margin guide line (`#dfa495`).

### 4. Checkboxes & Task Trackers
- **Checkboxes:** Replaced by square classical scholar boxes: 16px × 16px, 1px sepia border (`#4a3b32`).
- **Checked State:** Rendered as an authentic hand-drawn ink diagonal cross or dip-pen checkmark in deep iron gall blue (`#1a2744`) or sage green (`#3f5945`) for completed assignments.

### 5. Chips & Marginalia Tags
- Styled as miniature paper library tabs or pinned slips.
- Off-white ground (`#faf6ee`) with a delicate sepia or sage bounding box.
- Newsreader small-caps typography with increased tracking.

### 6. Unique Specialty Components
- **The Brass Ribbon Marker:** A floating vertical bookmark anchored at the viewport top, rendered in woven deep burgundy or woven emerald green, acting as a quick-return or table-of-contents anchor.
- **Marginal Annotations:** Floating callouts in `annotation-italic` with fine dotted callout leader lines pointing directly to active agenda items.
