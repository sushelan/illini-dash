2Month, manual add, and rows with no date
What changed
The tab strip is five names now — Today · Week · Month · No date · Exams — so Late moves into the header pill, where it reads as a state rather than a place. That is the same count your popup already fits at 400px; the strips in 1a and 1b would be replaced by this one.
A month in 400px cannot carry titles, so it carries weight: a dot per deadline in the course's hue, dimmed once finished, and the day you tap opens its list underneath. Tapping the month name is how you would reach the full-view grid popup.css already draws at 1400px.
Add by hand is a + in the header — same place on every tab. The form follows core/manual.ts: course, title, kind, date, time, and the refusal printed beside the field it is about.
Undated rows get their own tab rather than a fold at the bottom of the list. They never age out, they stay out of the badge, and each one offers the only three things that can end it: give it a date, tick it off, hide it.

1Illini Dash — 400px popup, soft card direction
What I assumed
Palette, course hues and the app mark are lifted from your existing ui.css tokens and icon-src/dash.svg, and the glyphs are the real paths from src/ui/icons.ts. The change is shape, not colour: rounded white cards on the blue ground instead of ruled rows, and more air per item.
Urgency is text — in 4h next to the clock, red once it is past. No colour-coded edges by default — Urgency edge in Tweaks turns them on, and Source names drops the source word from every row.
Coverage lives in a footer strip that is always there: "8 sources · synced 2m ago". It turns amber and grows a sentence when a source stops answering, which is the only time it asks for anything.
Grouping is by time, Today / Tomorrow / This week, with courses identified by a dot + code rather than a fill. Source is a quiet word under the title.
Content is real — titles, courses and shapes from your fixtures and preview data, including the awkward ones (merged two-source rows, an unbooked CBTF exam, an assumed time from a course site).
